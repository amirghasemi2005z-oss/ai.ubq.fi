# Posted Salary Ranges for the Title AI Engineer — 15 Public Job Postings

## METHOD

How the postings were found:

- Each employer's own careers site and official job board was searched directly for open postings whose title contains "AI Engineer" or "Machine Learning Engineer". No job aggregators or third-party listing sites were used; every posting below sits on the employer's own careers page or the employer's own official job board.
- Each candidate posting was opened on the employer's own page and read in full. A posting was kept only if (a) its title contains "AI Engineer" or "Machine Learning Engineer", (b) the location listed on the page is in Colorado, California, Washington, or New York, and (c) the page itself states a salary range for the role.
- Counting rule for multi-location postings: a posting is counted once, under the first eligible location listed on its page. When a page states different salary ranges for different locations or regions, the range the page assigns to the counted location is the one reported in the table.
- Pay basis: every posting in this set states an annual salary range (a yearly range as presented on the page); no hourly-rate postings are included.
- Work arrangement is reported from the posting's own statements (for example a stated hybrid policy, a workplace-type field, or a hybrid/remote tag on the page). Where the page states no arrangement, the cell reads "not shown".
- Posting date is reported only when the posting page itself displays a publication date; otherwise the cell reads "not shown".
- Salary figures, titles, and locations are transcribed exactly as written on each page, including the page's own punctuation. The SOURCES section reproduces a verbatim excerpt of each posting containing its title and salary range word-for-word.
